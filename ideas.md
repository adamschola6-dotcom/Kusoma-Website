# Kusoma Design Direction

## Theme Name
Night Lab / Soft Signal

## Intro
A premium dark-mode product story that feels like a quiet command center for focus: high-contrast editorial type, luminous study signals, and a tactile device mockup floating in deep space.

## Design movement
Move from dense, quiet darkness into a bright, tactile product surface, then settle into frosted feature modules. Motion should feel intentional and physics-aware rather than decorative.

## Core principles
1. One hero object: the Pixel is the visual anchor.
2. Keep the interface calm: a few strong signals over noisy decoration.
3. Use depth to explain product behavior: overlay cards should feel like they hover above the phone surface.
4. Every scroll state should add meaning, not just movement.

## Color philosophy
- Ink: `#0B1012` and `#11191C` for the deep field.
- Paper: `#F2F0E8` for editorial text and light panels.
- Signal cyan: `#91E5E5` for AI guidance and interaction.
- Signal lilac: `#C7B9FF` for brand energy and product chrome.
- Acid lime: `#D3F36B` for status, progress, and primary calls to action.
- Coral: `#FF7E69` reserved for playful micro-accents.

## Layout paradigm
A centered max-width editorial grid with a fixed nav, oversized hero type, a long sticky showcase stage, and a two-column feature section. The page uses generous negative space and fine-lined technical labels to balance the soft gradients.

## Signature elements
- A two-shape Nyota Tech constellation mark.
- Pill-shaped metadata tags with hairline borders.
- A high-detail CSS 3D Pixel mockup.
- A lavender Kusoma overlay card floating above the phone screen.
- `SCROLL TO EXPLORE` rail and progress bar.

## Interaction philosophy
Interactions are low-friction and tactile: scroll drives the narrative, pointer movement adds a small amount of phone tilt, nav links jump to story beats, and the product CTA is a quiet invitation rather than a hard sell.

## Animation
Hero particles breathe slowly. The scroll controller maps page progress into phone rotation, translation, scale, glow, and haze. Cards fade/slide in after the product is understood. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the continuously running loop and uses stable transforms.

## Typography system
Use a high-contrast modern grotesk stack: `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif. Headings are tight and heavy with negative tracking; labels are all-caps, monospace-like, and spaced for a technical voice.

## Brand essence
Kusoma means reading/studying; Nyota means star. The identity is about a small, reliable signal that helps a learner find their way through dense material without leaving the context they are already in.

## Brand voice
Clear, warm, quietly confident, and precise. Use short phrases and active verbs: "Ask in place", "Keep your thread", "Study without switching".

## Wordmark / logo
Use the Nyota constellation mark with a custom `NYOTA / TECH` wordmark in the header, and the Kusoma wordmark in the hero. The icon is a full-bleed ink square with a lilac/cyan constellation shape and a lime star.

## Signature brand color
Signal lilac `#C7B9FF`, supported by signal cyan and acid lime.
